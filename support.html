Support & Contact

Need help with My Shopping Lists? We're happy to hear from you. The quickest way to reach the developer is by email.

Email support

wilkoenterprises@outlook.com

When writing, it helps to include your device model, app version, what you expected to happen and what happened instead. Please don't include passwords, sign-in links or private group codes.

Common questions

Phone synchronisation: Group Phone Sync is a Premium feature. Check that each phone is signed in as intended and that the lists you want to share are selected.

Subscriptions: Your purchase and renewal settings are managed through Google Play. Open Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions to review them.

Photos: Photos added to items and people remain on the phone where they were added and do not synchronise between devices.

More guidance: Open the app's Settings → Help & How To section.

Privacy and account requests

For questions about personal information, account access or data removal, email wilkoenterprises@outlook.com. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.
