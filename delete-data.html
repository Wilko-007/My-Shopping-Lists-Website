PRIVACY & YOUR CHOICES

Request deletion of your data

My Shopping Lists is an independently developed Android app. You may request deletion of personal information associated with your use of the app, whether you use the Free or Premium version. This page explains how to make a request and what can be deleted or retained.

How to request deletion

  1. Email wilkoenterprises@outlook.com with the subject My Shopping Lists – Data Deletion Request.
  2. Tell us whether you want to delete an account (if your app version offers one), information held by our app services, or both. If applicable, provide the email address associated with the app or another non-sensitive identifier needed to locate your records.
  3. We will reply with any necessary identity-verification steps and clarify the data covered. Do not send passwords, payment card details or identity documents unless a secure verification method is specifically arranged.
  4. After verification, we will process eligible deletion requests for data within our control and explain any data that cannot be deleted, or must be retained.

Email a deletion request

What data may be deleted

  • Applicable app account, authentication and profile records held in our backend, if those features were used and the records can be identified.
  • Eligible synchronised shopping-list or planning information stored on our backend, where phone synchronisation was enabled in an app version you used.
  • Personal information we hold for support requests, subject to any necessary business or legal retention.
  • Eligible records associated with third-party service providers where we are authorised to request or perform deletion.

Deletion of an app account or backend data does not automatically cancel a Google Play subscription. Manage active subscriptions through Google Play separately.

Data held only on your device

Shopping lists, gift plans, gift-card records, budgets, notes and attached photos may be stored locally on your Android device. We generally cannot delete device-only files remotely. You can remove the information within the app where supported; if necessary, Android app-storage removal or uninstalling the app may delete locally stored data. Back up anything you wish to keep first.

Do not clear the app or uninstall it merely to make a deletion request; contact us first if you are unsure which information is stored locally versus online.

Information that may be retained

Some records may need to be retained where required or permitted for legal, financial, security, fraud-prevention, billing or dispute-resolution purposes. Google Play and payment providers may retain transaction history independently under their own obligations. App advertising, consent and purchase service providers can also have separate retention and deletion processes.

Retention periods depend on the record type, the service provider and any applicable legal obligation; there is no single fixed period covering all records. We will explain any relevant retention basis and period, where it can be determined, in our response to your request. Data is not promised to be automatically deleted within 90 days.

Questions or further privacy information

Contact wilkoenterprises@outlook.com for help with access, correction or deletion. See our Privacy Policy for information about the app and its service providers.
