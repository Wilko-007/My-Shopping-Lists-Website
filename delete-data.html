PRIVACY & YOUR CHOICES

Delete your account and data

My Shopping Lists is an independently developed Android app. This page explains how to delete your shared-lists account and the information linked to it, whether you use the Free or Premium version, and what is deleted or kept.

How to delete your shared-lists account

  1. Email wilkoenterprises@outlook.com with the subject My Shopping Lists – Delete my shared lists data.
  2. Tell us how to find your account: the email address you added in the app (Shared lists, then Add my email), or your phone's name on shared lists and roughly when you last used it.
  3. We will reply to confirm the request, and may ask a simple question to check it is yours. Do not send passwords, payment card details or identity documents.
  4. We delete your account and the data below, and confirm by email. Deletion is completed within 30 days of confirming your request.

Email a deletion request

What we delete

  • Your anonymous shared-lists account.
  • Your phone's name on shared lists.
  • Your email address, if you added one.
  • Your memberships in shared lists, and your Premium record on our server.
  • Shared lists and shared gift cards that you own, including their items.
  • Older app versions: any account or synchronised list data from earlier phone-sync features that we can identify.
  • Information we hold for your support requests, unless we must keep it for legal reasons.

Deleting your shared-lists account does not cancel or refund a Google Play purchase. Premium stays with your Google account and can be restored in the app.

What may be kept

  • Items you added to other people's shared lists stay in those lists for the people in them, but are no longer linked to your name.
  • Google Play and RevenueCat keep purchase records for billing, tax and legal reasons under their own retention rules.
  • Advertising and consent providers (Google AdMob and UMP) have their own retention and deletion processes.
  • We may keep a short note that a deletion request was completed (date only), and records we must keep for legal, security or fraud-prevention reasons.

Data held only on your phone

Your own lists, gift plans, gift-card records, budgets, notes, photos and settings are stored on your phone, not on our server, so we cannot delete them remotely. You can delete them in the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything stored on that phone. Back up anything you wish to keep first.

If you are unsure what is stored on your phone and what is on our server, contact us before uninstalling.

Questions or further privacy information

Contact wilkoenterprises@outlook.com for help with access, correction or deletion. See our Privacy Policy for information about the app and its service providers.
