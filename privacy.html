Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes information that may be processed when you use the My Shopping Lists Android app or this website. My Shopping Lists is independently developed in Australia. For enquiries, email wilkoenterprises@outlook.com.

Information used by the app

The app lets you create shopping lists, gift plans, budgets, gift-card records and to-do lists. The information you enter may be stored on your device. Depending on the app version and whether optional phone synchronisation is available and enabled, selected list information and authentication details may be processed by backend services to provide synchronisation. Phone synchronisation is disabled in the v2.2.4 release candidate; the functionality of earlier installed versions may differ.

Where sign-in features are enabled, authentication may involve your email address and related technical information. Please avoid adding sensitive information to list names or notes unless necessary.

Services used to operate the app

The app uses third-party services that may process information needed to provide their functions:

  • Supabase is used for backend functionality when enabled. Phone synchronisation is disabled in the v2.2.4 release candidate, while previously installed versions may differ.
  • Google Play for app distribution, subscriptions and billing.
  • RevenueCat for Premium purchase validation, entitlement status and purchase management. RevenueCat advertising-event measurement is disabled in the current production candidate.
  • Google AdMob for advertising in the free app. Depending on applicable privacy choices and SDK processing, this can involve device or other identifiers, approximate location, ad interactions, diagnostics and fraud-prevention information.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain applicable advertising consent and privacy preferences. Choices may be stored on your device and communicated as relevant consent signals to Google's advertising services.

Each service may process data under its own privacy terms. The exact data categories depend on the features used and the service configuration.

Advertising is not displayed to users whose Premium entitlement is verified. The app may withhold ads while entitlement or consent status is unresolved. Where required, an advertising privacy-options entry allows users to review or change applicable choices. Choosing not to allow certain data uses does not necessarily mean all advertising stops; Google may serve ads under applicable restrictions.

See Google's Privacy Policy and RevenueCat's Privacy Policy for provider information. The information processed can vary by app version, region, consent choices and provider practices.

Photos and shared lists

Photos attached to items and people are intended to remain on the device where they were added rather than synchronise with other phones. If you use an app version where group sharing is available and enabled, selected list content may be made available to chosen members of your group. Take care when deciding which information to share.

Optional website analytics

With your permission, this website uses Google Analytics 4 to measure page visits and clicks on Google Play links, and Metricool to measure website visits and pages viewed. Google Analytics may process information about your browser, approximate location, device and interactions. Metricool's website tracker makes requests to its service and uses your IP address to infer your country; Metricool states this tracker does not use cookies or store visitors' IP addresses. Neither analytics service is loaded unless you choose “Allow analytics”. Your choice is saved in your browser, and you can change it using the button below. If you previously allowed Google-only analytics, you will be asked to choose again before Metricool is enabled. Declining optional analytics does not affect access to the website.

Website

This promotional website provides app information and links to Google Play. It does not currently require a website account or a contact form. Your browser and the hosting provider may process standard technical information needed to serve the website, such as IP addresses and request logs.

Security, retention and requests

We aim to protect information using appropriate technical measures, but no internet service is completely risk-free. Information may be retained for as long as needed to provide the service, resolve support requests or meet legal obligations. Retention and deletion can also depend on third-party provider systems.

To ask about access, correction or deletion of your information, see our Data deletion request page or email wilkoenterprises@outlook.com. We may ask for information to verify the request.

Changes

We may update this notice as features or providers change. The date above will be revised when changes are published.
