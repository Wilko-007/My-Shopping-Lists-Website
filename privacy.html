Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes information that is processed when you use the My Shopping Lists Android app or this website. My Shopping Lists is independently developed in Australia. For enquiries, email wilkoenterprises@outlook.com.

Information that stays on your phone

Your shopping lists, gift plans, budgets, gift-card records, to-do lists, notes and settings are stored on your phone. Photos you attach to items or people are never uploaded: they stay on the phone where they were added, including in shared lists. Budgets also stay on each phone. Please avoid adding sensitive information to list names or notes.

Shared lists (optional)

If you share a list or join one with an invite code, the app creates an anonymous account for shared lists on our server. We do not ask for your name, email or a password to do this. To provide shared lists we store:

  • an anonymous account ID for your phone;
  • the name you choose for your phone (for example “Mum's phone”), which is shown to the people in your shared lists;
  • the content of the lists and gift cards you share, including list names, items, notes, gift-person names, gift-card retailer, card number, balance, expiry and spending, and who added, ticked or spent each one;
  • which shared lists you belong to and whether you are the owner or a member, and the list's group password in protected (hashed) form, never in readable form;
  • whether your account has Premium, confirmed with RevenueCat.

A shared list is visible only to people who join with its invite code. Anyone who has the code can join until the owner makes a new code or removes them, so only give the code to people you trust. Owners can remove people, change the code and group password, and stop sharing. Members can leave at any time.

Optional email: if you choose to add your email address, we use it only to send you a one-time code so you can keep your shared lists when you change phone. It is never shown to other people and is not used for marketing.

Bot protection: when shared lists are first set up on a phone, a Cloudflare Turnstile check runs, usually invisibly, to stop automated abuse. Cloudflare processes technical information such as your IP address and device and browser signals for this purpose.

Services used to operate the app

The app uses third-party services that process information needed to provide their functions:

  • Supabase hosts the shared-lists server and anonymous accounts. Information is encrypted in transit and protected by access rules so only the people in a list can read it. Supabase servers may be located outside Australia.
  • Resend sends the one-time sign-in code emails if you add an email address.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile for bot protection when shared lists are set up.
  • Google Play for app distribution and billing.
  • RevenueCat for Premium purchase validation, entitlement status, purchase management and purchase-related analytics. If you use shared lists, your RevenueCat customer ID is set to your anonymous shared-lists ID so our server can confirm Premium. RevenueCat advertising-event measurement is disabled.
  • Google AdMob for advertising in the free app. Depending on applicable privacy choices and SDK processing, this can involve device or other identifiers, approximate location, ad interactions, diagnostics and fraud-prevention information. The app requests non-personalised ads.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain applicable advertising consent and privacy preferences. Choices may be stored on your device and communicated as relevant consent signals to Google's advertising services.
  • Open Food Facts: if you scan a product barcode and look it up, the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts to find product details. That service receives the request and network information such as your IP address. Your lists and photos are not included.
  • Store logos are downloaded from retailer, image-hosting or Google favicon services, which receive the network information needed to send the image.
  • Google ML Kit recognises barcodes on your phone. Google may receive SDK usage and performance information; camera images are not uploaded.

Each service processes data under its own privacy terms. The exact data categories depend on the features used and the service configuration.

Advertising is not displayed to users whose Premium entitlement is verified. The app may withhold ads while entitlement or consent status is unresolved. Where required, an advertising privacy-options entry allows users to review or change applicable choices. Choosing not to allow certain data uses does not necessarily mean all advertising stops; Google may serve ads under applicable restrictions.

See Google's Privacy Policy and RevenueCat's Privacy Policy for provider information. The information processed can vary by app version, region, consent choices and provider practices.

Optional website analytics

With your permission, this website uses Google Analytics 4 to measure page visits and clicks on Google Play links, and Metricool to measure website visits and pages viewed. Google Analytics may process information about your browser, approximate location, device and interactions. Metricool's website tracker makes requests to its service and uses your IP address to infer your country; Metricool states this tracker does not use cookies or store visitors' IP addresses. Neither analytics service is loaded unless you choose “Allow analytics”. Your choice is saved in your browser, and you can change it using the button below. If you previously allowed Google-only analytics, you will be asked to choose again before Metricool is enabled. Declining optional analytics does not affect access to the website.

Website

This promotional website provides app information and links to Google Play. It does not currently require a website account or a contact form. Your browser and the hosting provider may process standard technical information needed to serve the website, such as IP addresses and request logs.

Security, retention and requests

We aim to protect information using appropriate technical measures, including encryption in transit and access controls, but no internet service is completely risk-free.

Shared-list information is kept while the list is shared. When the owner stops sharing a list, its contents are removed from our server. If you leave a list, your membership is removed; items you added stay in that list for the other people in it. Anonymous accounts, phone names and any email address are kept until you ask us to delete them. Some information may also be kept by service providers under their own retention rules, or where we must keep it for legal, billing or security reasons.

You can change your phone's name in the app (Shared lists, then Change my name). To delete your shared-lists account and the information linked to it, or to ask about access or correction, see our Data deletion request page or email wilkoenterprises@outlook.com. We may ask for information to verify the request, and aim to respond within 30 days.

Changes

We may update this notice as features or providers change. The date above will be revised when changes are published.
